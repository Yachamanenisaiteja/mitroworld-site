3. How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Messages and files: until you or the other people in the chat delete the account, or content is removed by moderation.
  • Meeting audio: deleted immediately after notes are written (normally within minutes).
  • Location: ignored after 24 hours and deleted when you turn off “Visible nearby”.
  • Safety and legal records: as long as needed to protect users and meet legal obligations, typically up to 180 days, or longer only where the law requires.

4. Your rights and choices

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU/UK GDPR and similar laws, you can access, correct and erase your data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to exercise your rights. Most choices are in the app (Settings → privacy switches, profile, Delete my account). For anything else, email saiteja46@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India or your local data protection authority.

5. Security

End-to-end encryption for one-to-one messages, TLS for all traffic with certificate pinning, encrypted storage on your phone, strict access rules on our servers, and independent security testing. No system is perfect; if we learn of a breach affecting you we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

6. Children

MITRO is not for children under 13 (16 in the EEA/UK). Where local law, such as India's DPDP Act for users under 18, requires verifiable parental consent, users must have it. We do not knowingly collect data from children below these ages and delete it if we learn of it. See our Child Safety Standards.

7. Changes

We will tell you in the app before important changes take effect.
