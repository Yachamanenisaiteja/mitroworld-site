Common questions

I didn't get my sign-in code

Check the number and country code, wait 30 seconds and tap “Resend OTP”, or sign in with email instead.

Can MITRO read my messages?

Not your one-to-one text messages — they are end-to-end encrypted on your phone. Compare the safety number in the chat's “Verify chat” screen to be sure. Group messages, photos and files are encrypted in transit and at rest and checked for adult content.

How do AI meeting notes work?

In a call, tap ✨. Everyone sees “AI notes ON”. When the call ends, a summary with decisions and action items appears in the chat, and the recording is deleted. Available with MITRO Pro.

How much does MITRO Pro cost?

The price is shown in your currency on the subscription screen and is set fairly for each country. Cancel anytime in your App Store or Google Play account.

Someone is bothering me

Open the chat menu → Block user or Report user, or long-press a message → Report. Reports are private.

I got a new phone

Sign in with the same number. For your security, older end-to-end encrypted messages can't be read on a new phone; your contacts will see that your security code changed.
