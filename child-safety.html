Child Safety Standards

MITRO by Yachamaneni Saiteja · Last updated: 18 November 2026

MITRO has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and any sexualisation of minors. These standards apply to everything on MITRO: messages, photos, files, profile photos, names, group chats and video calls.

What is prohibited

  • Creating, sharing, requesting or linking to CSAM in any form, including drawn, AI-generated or edited images.
  • Grooming, sexual conversations with minors, sextortion, or arranging to meet a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Any sexual content at all — MITRO bans all adult (18+) content for every user.

How we prevent and detect it

  • Every photo and profile picture is automatically checked for sexual content before anyone can see it; flagged photos are blocked and the sender receives a strike (3 strikes = suspension).
  • Explicit-word filters for messages, names and group names.
  • In-app reporting on every message, user and call, with a dedicated “Involves a child / minor safety” reason.
  • Accounts reported for sexual content by several people are suspended automatically pending human review.
  • Blocking, privacy controls, and no anonymous or random-stranger chat.

How we respond

  1. Reports about minor safety are prioritised for human review within 24 hours.
  2. CSAE content is removed and the account is banned permanently.
  3. We preserve required evidence and report to the relevant authorities — in India the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and law enforcement, and to NCMEC or other national authorities where required by law.
  4. We cooperate with lawful requests from law enforcement.

How to report

In the app: long-press the message or open the chat menu → Report → “Involves a child / minor safety”. Or email our child safety contact: saiteja46@gmail.com.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local police (in India dial 112; Childline 1098).

Child safety point of contact

Yachamaneni Saiteja (Child Safety Contact), Yachamaneni Saiteja — saiteja46@gmail.com. This contact is ready to receive notifications from Google Play and Apple about CSAE content.

MITRO complies with applicable child safety laws, including India's POCSO Act and IT Act.
