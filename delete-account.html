Delete your MITRO account

MITRO by Yachamaneni Saiteja

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open MITRO and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm twice. Your account is deleted immediately.

Can't open the app?

Email saiteja46@gmail.com from any address with the subject “Delete my MITRO account” and the phone number or email you use to sign in. We will verify it's you with a one-time code and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (phone/email), profile, username and profile photo
  • Contact fingerprints, location, encryption keys, push tokens and settings
  • The text and files of every message you sent (other people see “Message removed”)
  • Your memberships in all chats and groups

What we keep, and for how long

  • Anonymous blockchain fingerprints (they contain no personal data and cannot identify you).
  • Safety records about reports and suspensions, kept up to 180 days or longer only if the law requires.
  • Purchase records held by Apple/Google under their own policies.

If you have MITRO Pro, cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions — deleting the account does not cancel the store subscription.
